# Closed test

Google needs at least 12 testers opted in for 14 days in a row. Invite 16 or more, because anyone who opts out stops counting. An iPhone can't be used to test.

## Message to testers

```
Hi! I've built an Android version of Scripture Memory, my Bible verse memorization app, and Google needs people to test it for 14 days before it can go on the Play Store. Could you help?

It takes a minute to join, and it's free:
1. Tell me the Gmail address on your Android phone.
2. Once I've added you, open this link and tap "Become a tester": [PASTE OPT-IN LINK]
3. Install Scripture Memory from the Play Store link on that page.
4. Keep it installed for 14 days. Please don't leave the test early.

Try a few minutes of Daily Review when you can, plus a pack, a quiz and a reminder. If anything looks wrong, tell me what happened and on what phone.

Thank you!
```
