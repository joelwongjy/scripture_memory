## Full description (4,000 characters max)

```
Memorize Scripture a few minutes a day. Scripture Memory shows each verse again just before you'd forget it, so what you learn stays with you.

487 VERSES IN 15 PACKS
Learn from The Navigators' Topical Memory System, including TMS 60 and TMS 180, along with Design for Discipleship and 5 Assurances. Start from the beginning, or pick up where you stopped.

A DAILY REVIEW YOU CAN TRUST
The app works out which verses are due. Rate each one Again, Hard, Good or Easy, and it schedules the next review. If you've used Anki, this will feel familiar. New cards and reviews are counted separately, with daily limits you control.

PRACTICE YOUR WAY
• Type the first letter of each word
• Type every word
• Type the whole verse and see exactly what you missed
• Say it out loud

Hold to peek when you're stuck, or take a hint one word at a time.

EVERYTHING ELSE YOU NEED
• Search any verse by reference or words
• Daily streaks and a calendar of the days you studied
• Reminders at the time you choose
• Home screen widgets for your verse and progress
• NIV 1984, or NIV 2011 for TMS 60
• Light and dark mode

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads and no tracking. Your verses and progress stay on your device. The app sends anonymous usage statistics, never your verses, and you can turn that off in Settings.

Verse selections are based on The Navigators' Topical Memory System®. This app is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by The Navigators.
```
