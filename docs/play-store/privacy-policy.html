Privacy Policy

Updated November 2, 2026

Scripture Memory is designed to protect your privacy. We do not collect personal data, and the anonymous usage statistics we gather can't be linked to you.

Data collection

The app has no accounts, no advertising, and no third-party tracking. It collects no personal information.

Anonymous usage statistics

To help improve the app, it sends anonymous usage signals to TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service. These describe how the app is used, such as which screens you open and whether a review was finished. They never include your verses, search terms, or personal details. Your device's identifier is hashed before anything is sent, so the signals can't be linked to you, and they are never used for tracking or advertising.

You can turn this off at any time in the app, under Settings > Share Anonymous Usage.

Dictation

If you choose to dictate a verse, the app uses the speech recognition built into your phone, and asks for microphone access the first time. Your voice is handled by your phone's speech service, which may be provided by Apple or Google. The app doesn't record or keep your voice, and doesn't send it to us.

Verse updates

The app may check our server for corrections to verse text and download them. These requests contain no personal data.

Your data stays with you

Your memorization progress, settings, and reminders are stored on your device. On iPhone, if you enable iCloud, this data syncs across your devices through your personal Apple iCloud account. On Android, it can be included in your device's backup to your Google account if backup is turned on. We have no access to either. This data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy or Google's Privacy Policy.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, notifications are scheduled locally on your device. No notification data is sent to us or anyone else.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes its on-device data. On iPhone, iCloud-synced data can be managed or removed from Settings > [your name] > iCloud. On Android, backups can be managed in your device's Settings or your Google account. To ask about anonymous usage statistics, email us at the address below.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
